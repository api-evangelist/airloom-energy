# Airloom Energy

> Airloom Energy is a Laramie, Wyoming wind-energy hardware company engineering next-generation
> utility-scale turbines built on a low-profile track-and-wing architecture instead of a conventional
> tower. It targets low-cost, mass-manufacturable, high-energy-density generation for low-wind,
> height-restricted and hard-to-access sites, and is backed by Breakthrough Energy Ventures,
> Lowercarbon Capital, MCJ, Crosscut and Woven Earth.

## What the company is

Airloom builds physical generation equipment. Its published technical roadmap: kilowatt-scale
prototype (2023), pilot design (2024), pilot operations with power-production and CAPEX validation
(2025), commercial demonstration system (2027). It is designed and built in Laramie, Wyoming
(5452 Aerospace Drive, Laramie, WY 82070).
